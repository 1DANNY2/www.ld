Common questions

Before you reach out.

How long does a website take to build?

Most single-page sites launch within one to two weeks. Multi-page business sites usually take two to four weeks, and online stores take four to six, depending on how many products you're listing.

Do I own the website once it's finished?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the site and its content are yours. We can hand over the files and hosting login, or continue managing hosting for you if you'd rather not deal with it.

What if I need changes after the site is live?

Every package includes a free support period after launch. After that, small changes are billed hourly, or you can move to an ongoing maintenance plan if you expect regular updates.

Do you build all kinds of websites, or just certain types?

All kinds — business sites, online stores, restaurants, portfolios, blogs, booking systems, churches and schools, and personal or event sites. If you're not sure what you need, tell us about your business and we'll recommend the right fit.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope — see the packages above for starting prices. Every project begins with a free consultation so you get an exact quote before committing to anything.

Can you help with hosting and a domain name?

Yes. We can register your domain, set up hosting, and handle the technical setup so you don't have to figure it out yourself.
